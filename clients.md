## Lead Pipeline

### Status Legend
- **Prospect** - Identified, not yet contacted
- **Contacted** - Outreach sent, awaiting response
- **Engaged** - Conversation started
- **Demo Sent** - Personalized demo/shared
- **Negotiating** - Discussing price/requirements
- **Won** - Client confirmed, 50% advance collected
- **Delivered** - Website deployed
- **Completed** - Full payment received + testimonial collected
