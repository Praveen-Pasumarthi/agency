# Upsell Playbook

## When to Upsell

Upsell ONLY when:
1. The client's website is live and working well
2. They've expressed satisfaction with your work
3. The upsell provides genuine value to their business
4. You've built trust (wait at least 2-4 weeks after delivery)

---

## Upsell Menu

### Tier 1: Quick Wins (₹500 - ₹2,000/month)

**Website Maintenance**
- Price: ₹500 - ₹1,000/month
- What: Monthly updates, bug fixes, security patches
- Pitch: "I'll keep your website running smoothly so you can focus on your business."

**Hosting Management**
- Price: ₹200 - ₹500/month
- What: Domain renewal, hosting management, SSL
- Pitch: "I'll handle all the technical stuff — you never have to worry about your website going down."

**Content Updates**
- Price: ₹500 - ₹1,000/month
- What: Menu changes, photo updates, new pages
- Pitch: "Need to update your menu or add new photos? I'll handle it within 24 hours."

---

- Price: ₹2,000 - ₹5,000/month

- Pitch: "An AI assistant that answers customer questions even when you're asleep."

- Price: ₹5,000 - ₹8,000 one-time
- What: Automated replies, catalog, quick responses
- Pitch: "Automate your WhatsApp responses so you never miss a customer inquiry."

## Referral Program

### Offer
"Refer a friend and get 1 month of maintenance FREE" or "10% off your next project."

### Template

**Subject:** Quick favor?

Hi [Client Name],

Hope you're doing well! I'm currently taking on new clients and wanted to ask — do you know any other business owners who might need a website?

If you refer someone who signs up, I'll give you [1 month free maintenance / 10% discount / ₹1,000 credit].

No pressure at all — just thought I'd ask since you've been such a great client!

---

## Red Flags (Don't Upsell Yet)

- Client hasn't paid final payment yet
- Client has complained about the website
- Client is unresponsive
- Website has unresolved issues
- Less than 2 weeks since delivery
