# Outreach Strategy & Templates

## Rules

1. **Never spam** - Every message must be personalized
2. **Mention something specific** about their business
3. **Don't sell immediately** - Start a conversation
4. **Follow up** within 3-5 days if no response
5. **Maximum 3 follow-ups** per prospect
6. **Track everything** in clients.md

---

## Where to Find Leads

### Best Sources (English-speaking markets)
- **Google Maps** - Search "restaurants in Bengaluru", "gyms in Goa"
- **Instagram** - Location tags, food/fitness hashtags
- **Zomato / Swiggy** - Restaurant listings with ratings
- **JustDial** - Business directories
- **Google Search** - "[city] best restaurant", "[city] gym"

### Target Cities (English-friendly)
- Bengaluru (best - tech hub, English-speaking)
- Goa (tourist area, premium businesses)
- Hyderabad (HITEC City area)
- Pune (young demographic)
- Mumbai (South Mumbai premium areas)
- International (Dubai, Singapore, etc.)

---

## Lead Qualification Checklist

Only contact businesses that:
- [ ] Have NO website
- [ ] Have an OUTDATED website (built 3+ years ago)
- [ ] Have a POOR mobile experience
- [ ] Have NO online menu (restaurants)
- [ ] Have NO membership info online (gyms)
- [ ] Have good Google ratings (4+ stars)
- [ ] Are actively operating
- [ ] English-speaking management

## Objection Handling Playbook

### "We already have Instagram"

"That's great! Instagram is fantastic for engagement. But here's the thing — Instagram doesn't show up when someone Googles 'best restaurant near me.' A website helps you capture that search traffic.

Plus, you can't put a full menu, reservation system, or detailed pricing on Instagram. A website gives you a professional presence that Instagram alone can't provide."

### "We don't need a website"

"I completely understand. Most of my clients said the same thing before we worked together. Here's what changed their mind:

- 75% of customers judge a business by its website
- 88% of local business searches on mobile result in a call or visit within 24 hours
- Businesses with websites get 2x more leads than those without

Even a simple website can make a big difference. Would you like to see some examples?"

### "It's too expensive"

"I hear you. Let me ask — how much would one new customer be worth to you? If a website brings even 5 extra customers per month, it pays for itself quickly.

I also offer flexible payment plans. And I'm currently offering an introductory rate for new clients. Would you like to hear about it?"

### "We'll think about it"

"Of course! Take your time. I'll send you a quick demo so you can see what's possible — no obligation. If you decide to move forward later, I'll be here.

Can I send you the demo to your email or WhatsApp?"

### "I'll call you later"

"Sounds good! I'll send you a quick message with the demo link so you have it when you're ready. Feel free to reach out anytime."

### "We're too busy right now"

"I totally understand — running a business is full-on. That's actually why I offer a complete done-for-you service. You just approve the design, and I handle everything else.

Would it help if I create the demo first so you can see it when you have a spare moment?"

### "Can you do it for free?"

"I appreciate the honesty! Here's my approach — I create a free demo so you can see exactly what you'd get. If you love it, we discuss pricing. If not, no hard feelings.

Would you like me to create that demo?"

### "We already tried a website and it didn't work"

"I'm sorry to hear that. That's actually why I focus on what most web designers miss — speed, mobile experience, and conversion optimization. A beautiful website that loads slowly or doesn't guide visitors to take action won't help.

I'd love to show you the difference. Can I create a quick demo?"

## Response Handling

### Positive Response
"Great! I'll have a demo ready for you in 24 hours. What's the best email to send it to?"

### Asking for Price
"I'll include pricing in the demo. First, let me show you what you'd get so we can discuss the best package for your needs."

### Wants to Know More
"Happy to answer any questions! Here's a quick summary of what I offer:
- Modern, mobile-first website
- Fast loading (under 2 seconds)
- SEO optimized
- WhatsApp integration
- Google Maps
- Contact form
- 7-day delivery

Want me to create a demo for your business?"

### Not Interested
"No problem at all! If you ever change your mind, I'm here. Wishing you continued success with [Business Name]!"

### No Response After 3 Follow-ups
- Log in clients.md as "No Response"
- Move on to next prospect
- Can revisit in 3-6 months
