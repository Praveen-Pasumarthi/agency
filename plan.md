# Design Rules

Use premium typography.

Use generous whitespace.

Use subtle animations.

Avoid template-looking layouts.

Every section should feel custom built.

Think Apple, Stripe, Airbnb—not generic WordPress themes.

# Outreach Rules

Never spam.

Personalize every message.

Mention something specific about the business.

Example

Hi! I came across your restaurant and loved your food photos. I noticed you don't currently have a modern website where customers can view your menu or make reservations. I actually created a sample homepage to show what's possible for your business. I'd be happy to share it—no obligation.

Goal

Start conversations.

Do NOT immediately try to sell.
