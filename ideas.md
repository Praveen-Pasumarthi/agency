## Growth Roadmap

### Phase 1: Freelancing (Months 1-3)
- Land 5-10 website clients
- Build portfolio
- Establish pricing
- Revenue target: ₹50,000 - ₹1,00,000

### Phase 2: Small Agency (Months 4-6)
- Hire 1-2 freelancers
- Take on bigger projects
- Start maintenance plans
- Revenue target: ₹1,50,000 - ₹3,00,000/month

### Phase 3: Recurring Revenue (Months 7-12)
- Launch first SaaS product
- Convert website clients to SaaS users
- Build product-market fit
- Revenue target: ₹3,00,000 - ₹5,00,000/month

### Phase 4: Digital Business (Year 2+)
- Multiple SaaS products
- Full product suite for local businesses
- Team of 5-10
- Revenue target: ₹10,00,000+/month
